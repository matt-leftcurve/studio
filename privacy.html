yt-desc — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

yt-desc is a personal, single-user desktop tool used by the FULLPORTMATT channel owner to update the titles and descriptions of his own YouTube videos via the YouTube Data API.

  • It accesses only the channel owner's own YouTube account, with his explicit OAuth consent.
  • It stores OAuth tokens locally on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any third party.
  • It collects no data from any other person and has no users other than the owner.
